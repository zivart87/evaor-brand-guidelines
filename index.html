These guidelines define the visual identity of EvaOr and provide a foundation for using its brand elements consistently across communications.

Use these guidelines and the original brand assets when creating materials for EvaOr. Consistent application helps build a clear and recognisable brand presence.

The EvaOr logo is a central element of our visual identity. This section provides guidance on using it consistently across communications.

04EvaOr

The idea behind the logo

EvaOr begins with people and the challenges they face. We draw on our experience, knowledge and technology to create practical solutions — from automation and applications to complete digital projects.

The name brings two ideas together: Eva represents the human side of technology, while Or means “light” in Hebrew. The symbol is formed from individual elements, like particles of light coming together to illuminate an idea and give it form.

13EvaOr

Usage on backgrounds

Choose the logo version that provides clear contrast against its background.

Use the white logo on dark backgrounds and the dark logo on the brand gradient or light backgrounds. On photographs, select a clear area that keeps every element legible.

14EvaOr

Logo mark

The EvaOr brandmark can be used independently where a compact visual identifier is needed, such as social media avatars and app icons.

Use the brandmark in a rounded square or circle, with the approved black, gradient or light peach backgrounds.

Minimum brandmark height
Print: 10 mm
Digital: 64 px
